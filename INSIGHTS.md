# Final Insights & Business Intelligence

## 1. Customer Segmentation & Revenue Drivers
- **Mainstream Dominance:** The "Mainstream" segment is the 
  primary revenue engine, leading across both volume and value metrics.
- **High-Value Clusters:** Within the Mainstream segment, 
  **Retirees** and **Older Singles/Couples** exhibit the highest 
  purchase volume.
- **Premium Opportunities:** **Older Singles/Couples** represent 
  the dominant group within the Premium segment, indicating a strong 
  preference for high-end products in mature households.

## 2. Mix Delta Analysis
- **Positive Profitability Mix:** **Mainstream Young Singles/Couples 
  (+0.4 pp)** and **Mainstream Retirees (+0.1 pp)** show a positive 
  Mix Delta, indicating these customers purchase higher-value products 
  relative to their volume share.
- **Budget Segment Efficiency:** Budget segments consistently show 
  negative Mix Delta, suggesting high price sensitivity and a 
  preference for value brands or promotional formats.

## 3. Brand & Pack Size Performance
- **Category Leader:** **Doritos** leads with $14.4K in sales, 
  followed by **Smiths** ($11.1K), confirming strong brand equity 
  in the Australian market.
- **Hero Format:** The **175g** pack size is the top-selling format 
  with 11.9K units sold, the standard choice for both individual 
  and household consumption.
